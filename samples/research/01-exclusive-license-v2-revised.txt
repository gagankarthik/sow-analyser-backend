This Exclusive License Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of October 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Northfield University, an instrumentality of the State of Minnesota, acting through its Technology Commercialization Office, with offices at 1524 North High Street, Minneapolis, Minnesota 43201 ("University"), and Lakeshore BioSensors, Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1275 Kinnear Road, Minneapolis, Minnesota 43212 ("Licensee").

University owns certain inventions relating to a flexible electrochemical sensor for continuous lactate and glucose monitoring, developed in the laboratory of Dr. Elena Vasquez, Department of Biomedical Engineering, and described in the Licensed Patents. Licensee wishes to obtain, and University is willing to grant, an exclusive license under the Licensed Patents on the terms of this Agreement.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Licensed Patents" means U.S. Patent Application No. 63/412,557, filed March 3, 2025, titled "Flexible Electrochemical Biosensor for Continuous Metabolite Monitoring," and all patents issuing from it, together with any continuations, divisionals and foreign counterparts.

1.2 "Licensed Product" means any product, kit or service whose manufacture, use or sale would, but for this Agreement, infringe a Valid Claim of the Licensed Patents.

1.3 "Net Sales" means the gross amounts invoiced by Licensee and its Affiliates for Licensed Products, less customary trade discounts, returns, sales taxes and freight actually allowed or paid.

1.4 "Field of Use" means point-of-care and wearable monitoring of lactate and glucose in human sweat and interstitial fluid for sports medicine, critical care and diabetes management.

1.5 "Territory" means worldwide.

1.6 "Sublicense Income" means all consideration received by Licensee from a sublicensee in consideration of a sublicense under the Licensed Patents, other than running royalties on Net Sales.

2. LICENSE GRANT

2.1 University hereby grants to Licensee an exclusive, royalty-bearing license under the Licensed Patents, in the Field of Use and the Territory, to make, have made, use, sell, offer for sale and import Licensed Products.

2.2 University reserves for itself and for other academic and non-profit research institutions the right to practise the Licensed Patents for research, teaching and educational purposes. The license is subject to the rights of the United States Government under 35 U.S.C. 200-212.

2.3 The license includes the right to grant sublicenses in accordance with Article 4.

3. FEES, ROYALTIES AND EQUITY

3.1 License Issue Fee. Licensee shall pay University a non-refundable license issue fee of Seventy-Five Thousand Dollars ($75,000) within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date.

3.2 Equity. Within sixty (60) days after the Effective Date, Licensee shall issue to University shares of common stock of Licensee equal to five percent (5%) of its fully diluted capitalization, with anti-dilution protection until Licensee has raised $3,000,000 in equity financing.

3.3 Running Royalty. Licensee shall pay University a running royalty of three and one-half percent (3.5%) of Net Sales of all Licensed Products sold by Licensee or its Affiliates.

3.4 Milestone Payments. Licensee shall pay University the following milestone payments:
(a) Fifty Thousand Dollars ($50,000) upon enrollment of the first subject in a clinical validation study, anticipated by June 30, 2027;
(b) One Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($150,000) upon FDA 510(k) clearance of the first Licensed Product, anticipated by December 31, 2028;
(c) Two Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($250,000) upon the First Commercial Sale of a Licensed Product, anticipated by June 30, 2029.

3.5 Minimum Annual Royalty. Beginning with calendar year 2028, Licensee shall pay University a minimum annual royalty of Ten Thousand Dollars ($10,000), creditable against running royalties due for the same calendar year.

3.6 Sublicense Income. Licensee shall pay University twenty-five percent (25%) of all Sublicense Income received by Licensee.

4. SUBLICENSING

4.1 Licensee may grant sublicenses under the Licensed Patents with University's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Licensee shall deliver a copy of each sublicense to University within thirty (30) days after its execution.

4.2 Each sublicense shall be consistent with this Agreement and shall, at the sublicensee's option, survive termination of this Agreement with University as licensor.

5. DILIGENCE

5.1 Licensee shall use commercially reasonable efforts to develop and commercialize Licensed Products and shall meet the following diligence milestones:
(a) complete a working prototype of a Licensed Product by June 30, 2027;
(b) submit a 510(k) premarket notification to the FDA by June 30, 2028;
(c) make the First Commercial Sale of a Licensed Product by June 30, 2029.

5.2 If Licensee fails to meet any diligence milestone and does not cure that failure within ninety (90) days after written notice from University, University may terminate this Agreement or convert the license to non-exclusive.

6. REPORTS AND RECORDS

6.1 Licensee shall deliver a royalty report to University within forty-five (45) days after the end of each calendar quarter, showing Net Sales, royalties due and Sublicense Income received.

6.2 Licensee shall deliver an annual progress report on its development and commercialization of Licensed Products by January 31 of each year.

6.3 Licensee shall keep accurate records for three (3) years and permit University's independent auditor to inspect them once per calendar year on thirty (30) days' notice.

7. PATENT PROSECUTION AND OWNERSHIP

7.1 Title to the Licensed Patents remains with University. University shall control the prosecution and maintenance of the Licensed Patents using counsel of its choice, and Licensee shall reimburse University for reasonable patent costs incurred after the Effective Date.

7.2 Improvements made solely by University employees are owned by University. Improvements made solely by Licensee employees are owned by Licensee, and Licensee hereby grants to University a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to practise those improvements for research and educational purposes.
