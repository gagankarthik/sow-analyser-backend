FEDERAL SUBAWARD AGREEMENT

Huron Agreement No. AGR00012451
Workday Ref: WD-GR-220904 (Cost Center CC-ENG-4471)
OSU Subaward No. SUB-2026-0193

This Federal Subaward Agreement (the "Subaward") is entered into as of December 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between The Ohio State University, an instrumentality of the State of Ohio, as pass-through entity ("University"), and Lakeshore State University Research Foundation, a not-for-profit corporation ("Subrecipient").

University has received Prime Award No. W911NF-26-1-0042 from the U.S. Army Research Office (the "Prime Award") for the project "Resilient Autonomous Sensing Networks" under the direction of Dr. Kevin Mueller, Department of Computer Science and Engineering. University issues this Subaward to Subrecipient for the portion of the project described in Attachment 1 (the "Project"), under the direction of Dr. Sarah Lin at Subrecipient.

1. PERIOD OF PERFORMANCE AND FUNDING

1.1 The period of performance is December 1, 2026 through November 30, 2027.

1.2 University shall reimburse Subrecipient for allowable costs incurred in performing the Project, in a total amount not to exceed One Hundred Eighty Thousand Dollars ($180,000). Subrecipient shall invoice University monthly, and University shall pay each proper invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

1.3 Subrecipient shall submit its final invoice within sixty (60) days after the end of the period of performance.

2. REPORTS AND FLOW-DOWN TERMS

2.1 Subrecipient shall submit technical progress reports to University quarterly and a final technical report within sixty (60) days after the end of the period of performance.

2.2 The terms of the Prime Award flowed down to Subrecipient are listed in Attachment 2 by clause number, including FAR 52.204-21, FAR 52.222-50 and DFARS 252.204-7012, together with 2 CFR Part 200 as applicable to the Prime Award.

3. PUBLICATION

3.1 Subrecipient may publish the results of the Project. Subrecipient shall provide University and the Army Research Office a copy of each proposed publication thirty (30) days before submission.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 Each party retains ownership of its Background Intellectual Property and of inventions made solely by its own employees in performing the Project. Inventions are subject to the rights of the United States Government under 35 U.S.C. 200-212 and the patent rights clause of the Prime Award.

5. EXPORT CONTROL AND FOREIGN NATIONALS

5.1 Subrecipient shall comply with all applicable United States export control laws. Foreign nationals shall not participate in the Project or have access to Project data without the prior written approval of the Army Research Office contracting officer, and Subrecipient shall limit Project personnel to U.S. citizens and permanent residents.

6. LIABILITY

6.1 Each party shall be responsible for its own acts and omissions and those of its employees to the extent permitted by applicable law. Neither party shall indemnify the other.

7. GOVERNING LAW

7.1 This Subaward shall be governed by the laws of the State of Ohio and applicable federal law. Nothing in this Subaward shall be construed as a waiver of the sovereign immunity of University.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Subaward as of the Effective Date.
