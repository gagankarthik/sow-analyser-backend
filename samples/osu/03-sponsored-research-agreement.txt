SPONSORED RESEARCH AGREEMENT

Huron Agreement No. AGR00012402
Workday Ref: WD-GR-220871
OSU Office of Sponsored Programs Proposal No. PR-2026-08812

This Sponsored Research Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of November 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between The Ohio State University, an instrumentality of the State of Ohio, acting through its Office of Sponsored Programs, 1960 Kenny Road, Columbus, Ohio 43210 ("University"), and Midwest Advanced Materials Corp., a Michigan corporation with offices at 2100 Commonwealth Boulevard, Ann Arbor, Michigan 48105 ("Sponsor").

Sponsor wishes to support research at University on high-temperature ceramic matrix composites, to be conducted under the direction of Dr. Priya Raman (the "Principal Investigator"), Department of Materials Science & Engineering, College of Engineering.

1. RESEARCH

1.1 University shall use reasonable efforts to perform the research described in Exhibit A (the "Research") during the period from November 1, 2026 to October 31, 2028. The Principal Investigator shall direct the Research. University does not guarantee any particular result.

2. PAYMENT

2.1 Sponsor shall pay University a fixed price of Four Hundred Twenty-Five Thousand Dollars ($425,000) for the Research, in eight quarterly installments of $53,125 invoiced in advance. Each invoice is payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

3. REPORTS

3.1 The Principal Investigator shall provide Sponsor with written technical progress reports quarterly during the Research.

3.2 University shall provide Sponsor with a final report within ninety (90) days after the end of the Research.

4. PUBLICATION

4.1 University and the Principal Investigator may publish and present the results of the Research. University shall submit each proposed publication to Sponsor at least ninety (90) days before submission for publication, and Sponsor may require the removal of its Confidential Information during that period.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 Each party retains all right, title and interest in its Background Intellectual Property. Inventions made solely by University employees or students in performing the Research are owned by University; inventions made solely by Sponsor employees are owned by Sponsor; inventions made jointly are owned jointly.

5.2 University grants Sponsor a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use University inventions for internal research purposes and an option, exercisable within six (6) months after disclosure, to negotiate an exclusive, royalty-bearing license on commercially reasonable terms.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY AND USE OF NAMES

6.1 Each party shall hold the other party's Confidential Information in confidence for three (3) years after its disclosure. University retains the right to use all data generated in the Research for research, teaching and publication.

6.2 Neither party shall use the name, marks or logos of the other party in any advertising or publicity without the other party's prior written consent.

7. EXPORT CONTROL

7.1 The parties intend the Research to be fundamental research, the results of which are ordinarily published. Sponsor shall not provide University with any information or material subject to the Export Administration Regulations or the International Traffic in Arms Regulations without first giving University written notice and receiving University's written consent.

8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Sponsor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless University, its trustees, officers, employees and students from all claims arising from Sponsor's use of the results of the Research.

8.2 University shall indemnify and hold harmless Sponsor from any claims, losses or damages arising from University's performance of the Research.

9. GOVERNING LAW

9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Ohio. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as a waiver of the sovereign immunity of University or the State of Ohio.

10. TERM AND TERMINATION

10.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement on ninety (90) days' written notice. On termination Sponsor shall pay all costs incurred and all non-cancellable obligations made by University before the date of termination.

10.2 University shall submit its final invoice within sixty (60) days after the end of the Research.

11. GENERAL

11.1 This Agreement, with Exhibit A, is the entire agreement of the parties regarding the Research and may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
